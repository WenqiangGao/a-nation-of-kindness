THE QUESTION

What kind of
future are we building?

We are entering an extraordinary period of human history. Artificial intelligence and emerging technologies are transforming how we work, communicate, learn, create and make decisions.

But technological capability alone cannot determine the future we want. Human dignity, empathy, responsibility, respect and concern for others must remain part of the equation.

TECHNOLOGY FOR HUMANITY

Five principles for
a human-centred future.

Technology should be powerful and responsible. Innovation and humanity are not competing ideas.

01

Human Dignity

People are more than data points, users or components of technological systems.

02

Human Well-being

Innovation should contribute to human flourishing alongside efficiency and productivity.

03

Responsible Innovation

Safety, privacy, transparency, accountability and unintended consequences belong in the process from the start.

04

Human Connection

Technology should strengthen human potential and meaningful relationships.

05

Shared Benefit

Progress should create opportunities broadly and expand human potential.

BUSINESS FOR HUMANITY

Success and
responsibility
can coexist.

Kindness in business does not mean abandoning competition, profitability or performance. It means recognizing that long-term success and human responsibility can coexist.

  • Treat employees with dignity
  • Build products that genuinely improve lives
  • Support communities and mentor the next generation
  • Consider the human consequences of major decisions
