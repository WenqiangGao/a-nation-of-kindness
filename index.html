ABOUT THE MOVEMENT

Who
We Are

A Nation of Kindness is a Canadian movement initiated by a group of individuals who believe kindness should have a stronger place in how we live, work, lead, and build our communities.

Coming from diverse professional and community backgrounds, we share a simple belief: kindness is more than a personal virtue. It can shape the way we treat one another, conduct business, develop technology, strengthen communities, and create a better future.

A Nation of Kindness is designed to be open and inclusive. It does not belong to any one person, profession, organization, or community. It is an invitation for people across Canada — children, families, professionals, businesses, educators, technology leaders, and community organizations — to turn kindness into meaningful action.

We envision a Canada where kindness is reflected not only in everyday human interactions, but also in the decisions that shape our workplaces, businesses, technologies, institutions, and communities.

A society where success is measured not only by what we achieve, but also by how we treat people along the way — and where progress, leadership, and innovation remain grounded in humanity.

Together, we can help build a kinder, more connected, and more humane Canada.

OUR MISSION

Make Kindness
a Principle.

To make kindness a guiding principle not only in how we treat one another, but in how we lead, build, invest and innovate—helping create a more humane society and a more responsible future.

THE QUESTION

What kind of
future are we building?

We are entering an extraordinary period of human history. Artificial intelligence and emerging technologies are transforming how we work, communicate, learn, create and make decisions.

But technological capability alone cannot determine the future we want. Human dignity, empathy, responsibility, respect and concern for others must remain part of the equation.

TIER 2

Business
for Humanity

Business creates jobs, products, services, opportunity, and progress. Investment provides the capital that helps ideas grow.

But when profit becomes the only measure of success, business can lose sight of why it exists in the first place.

Business for Humanity invites entrepreneurs, executives, investors, and professionals to reconnect business and capital with human purpose.

Ask the Bigger Questions
  • Why does this business exist?
  • What real need does it serve?
  • What value are we creating?
  • Beyond financial return, how are people better because this business exists?
  • What are we funding?
  • Does our investment strengthen society, improve lives, or create long-term value?
  • How are we growing? Are employees, customers, communities, and future generations considered?
Take Part — or Take the Lead
  • Join or Initiate a Business Roundtable — Bring business leaders and investors together to discuss purpose, responsible growth, and the role of business in society.
  • Start a Purpose Conversation — Ask your leadership team, board, or investment committee: What are we building, and why does it matter?
  • Review One Business or Investment Decision — Look beyond return and consider its human, social, and long-term impact.
  • Mentor with Purpose — Help the next generation of entrepreneurs build businesses that create both economic and human value.
  • Share a Better Practice — Show how your company or investment approach balances performance with responsibility.
One Business. One Purposeful Action.

Choose one action that reconnects business with the people and purpose it is meant to serve.

Profit matters. But profit should be the result of creating real value — not the purpose by itself.

TIER 3

Technology
for Humanity

Technology is shaping our future. We believe it must remain safe, responsible, and firmly in service of humanity.

Take Part — or Take the Lead
  • Join or Initiate a Roundtable — Bring together technologists, researchers, professionals, and business leaders to discuss AI safety, human impact, and responsible innovation.
  • Join or Form a Working Group — Help develop practical principles, recommendations, and solutions for safer, more human-centred technology.
  • Attend or Organize a Forum — Create broader conversations around AI, society, business, policy, and the future of humanity.
  • Contribute Your Expertise — Share research, professional experience, case studies, or practical solutions.
  • Start a Conversation — Raise an important question in your workplace, university, professional network, or community.
  • Take One Action — Join an initiative — or create one.
Bring people together. Start a conversation. Share your expertise. Help turn concern about AI into meaningful action.
Technology should not only become more powerful. It should become safer, wiser, and more human.
